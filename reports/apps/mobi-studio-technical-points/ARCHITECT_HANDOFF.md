# Technical Points — Architect Handoff

The app is isolated under `apps/mobi-studio-technical-points/`. It reuses `Infrastructure`; it does not alter Foundation, Schema, Walls, Openings, RuleRunner, or any industrial layer.

The `technical:` type prefix identifies architectural technical records. `hostId` is the official HostSurfaceID. Category validation provides the connectivity contract without simulating equipment operation.
